# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts turn business and stakeholder needs into clear requirements and workflows that the delivery team can act on.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate requirements with stakeholders
- Define workflows, clarify acceptance criteria, and trace changes to requirements
- Surface gaps, assumptions, and dependencies before work begins

### Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers on scope and priorities; Product Managers retain product-prioritization ownership
- Work with Project Managers to communicate requirement changes and assess delivery impacts
- Clarify requirements with Developers and QA/Testing throughout implementation and validation

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity and rework
- Keep delivered work aligned with stakeholder needs and acceptance criteria

---

## Delivery Leads / Scrum Masters

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads, including Scrum Masters, facilitate the team's delivery process and help maintain a sustainable flow of work.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve blockers; escalate issues that need broader ownership
- Track flow and team commitments, making delivery risks visible

### Interactions
- Coordinate with Project Managers on milestones, dependencies, and escalations
- Work with Product Managers and Developers to keep prioritized work ready and manageable
- Support the team without taking ownership of product priorities or technical decisions

### Goals
- Improve collaboration and predictability
- Help the team deliver steadily while addressing impediments early

---

## Technical Leads / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide implementation and technical alignment while enabling Developers to deliver maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and communicate architecture trade-offs
- Support Developers through reviews, technical decisions, and risk identification
- Coordinate technical dependencies and uphold engineering standards

### Interactions
- Partner with Developers on implementation and code quality
- Work with Product Managers and Business Analysts to assess feasibility and clarify trade-offs
- Align with Project Managers on technical risks and dependencies; technical decisions remain with engineering

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable solutions
- Resolve technical uncertainty early and keep implementation aligned across the team

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads coordinate the test strategy and validation needed to provide confidence that delivery meets its quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test coverage and validation approaches for requirements and risks
- Coordinate QA/Testing activities and report defects and quality risks
- Confirm validation evidence against acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done

### Interactions
- Collaborate with Business Analysts and Product Managers to make requirements testable
- Work with Developers to plan testing, reproduce defects, and verify fixes
- Share quality and release-readiness information with Project Managers and Release Managers

### Goals
- Find issues early and reduce the risk of defects reaching users
- Make quality status and release risks visible to decision-makers

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and deployment activities so that approved changes reach users safely and with clear communication.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release schedule, readiness checklist, and deployment coordination
- Confirm that required testing, release notes, and rollback plans are in place
- Coordinate release communications and track post-deployment verification

### Interactions
- Align timing and stakeholder updates with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Confirm readiness with Technical Leads, QA Leads, Developers, and Support / Operations
- Coordinate deployment steps without replacing the team's release approval or change-control process

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and avoid surprises for users and stakeholders
- Make releases predictable, observable, and easier to recover if issues occur

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support / Operations Representatives bring user-support and production-operability perspectives into planning and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring support issues, production feedback, and operational constraints
- Advise on monitoring, runbooks, support readiness, and incident risks
- Report post-release observations and help route incidents or follow-up work

### Interactions
- Provide user and operational context to Product Managers, Business Analysts, and Project Managers
- Coordinate readiness and handoffs with Release Managers, QA Leads, and Developers
- Work with Technical Leads on observability, reliability, and incident follow-up

### Goals
- Improve operability and shorten the time to detect and resolve production issues
- Ensure releases are supportable and informed by real user and production feedback
